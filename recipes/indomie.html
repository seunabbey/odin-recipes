Indomie Noodles & Egg

A plate of Indomie Noodles & Egg

Indomie recipes are just too many in Nigeria, kids love them as well as adults. Indomie noodles have been able to capture the hearts of most Nigerians with a series of good products and of course their often captivating commercials.

Ingredients for Indomie Noodles & Egg

  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Cup Diced Carrots
  • 1/2 Cup Chopped Green bean
  • 200 g Noodles
  • Noodle Follow-come spices
